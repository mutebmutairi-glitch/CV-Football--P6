# Dataset card: SoccerNet role crops (Phase 1)

## Source and licence
- **Source:** SoccerNet Game State Reconstruction 2025 (SN-GSR-2025), `train` split, https://huggingface.co/datasets/SoccerNet/SN-GSR-2025
- **Licence:** GPL-3.0. The GSR clips do not require the SoccerNet NDA. No images are redistributed in this repository; crops are regenerated by the notebook.
- **Team-collected data:** none in Phase 1.

## Content
| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Sequences in archive / processed | 57 / 48 (30 s each, 25 fps) |
| Independent matches (`game_id`) | 3 (4, 6, 9) |
| Sampled frames | 1,440 (every 25th frame, max 35 per sequence) |
| Person boxes in sampled frames | 23,386 |
| Role crops after cleaning and caps | 7,752 |
| Classes | player, goalkeeper, referee (ball boxes are not used by the classifier) |

Why roles and not left/right team: the `left`/`right` label in GSR is the side of the pitch within one match, not a kit identity, so it cannot be learned across matches. Team A/B is assigned per clip by k-means on torso colour.

## Cleaning
| Check | Method | Removed |
|---|---|---|
| Corrupt / unreadable frames | Decode check on every sampled frame | 0 frames |
| Invalid geometry | Box must be non-empty and inside the image | 0 boxes |
| Frames without people | Frames with no retained box dropped | 0 frames |
| Tiny crops | Min side 24 px | 1,592 crops |
| Exact duplicates | SHA-256 of crop resized to 48x48 over 21,794 unique crops | 0 same-role, 0 conflicting |

## Label audit
200 random crops (all splits, seed 42) reviewed manually, 0 errors. Exact Clopper-Pearson 95% upper bound on the label error rate: 1.83% (two-sided), 1.49% (one-sided). Audited IDs saved in `label_audit_200.csv`. (Earlier pilot: 50 crops, 0 errors.)

## Preprocessing and augmentation
Resize to 224x224, ImageNet normalisation. Training only: horizontal flip, colour jitter (brightness/contrast/saturation 0.15, hue 0.04).

## Limitations and privacy
- Only three independent matches, so one match per split; results may not generalise to other kits, leagues or stadiums.
- Goalkeepers are rare (194 training crops) and their kits change every match.
- Broadcast camera only, with close-ups, motion blur and occlusion.
- Ball vs player imbalance is extreme; it is handled in the Phase 2 detector.
- People shown are players and referees in published broadcast footage covered by the dataset licence. No personal data is collected and no images are committed.
